# Changelog

Pitlane HQ is in **beta**. The phone apps share their version number with PitlaneHQ.exe and the
web (`version.txt`): `0.MINOR.PATCH`. A MINOR version is a set of new features; fixes ride along as
"Fixes and improvements". 1.0.0 comes when the beta ends. Newest first.

## 0.9.10 beta

- **Recent records in Community**: the fastest lap of every car and track, the newest first, who set it and whom it
  beat; a tap opens its leaderboard. The track page (map, record per car) is in development.
- **Changes from your PC or the web reach the phone at once**: the server tells the app the moment another device
  saves the account. Nobody syncs by hand: the Sync button is gone.
- **The discipline symbols show on iPhone** (licence summary, Community, leagues).
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.9.9 beta

- **The coach's advice follows your data**: no "brake later" when you already brake later than the reference, no
  "full throttle sooner" when you are already on the throttle earlier. The track map runs along the reference lap's
  path, so the two lines are the right way round, and a lap without a path of its own (an older one, a rival's)
  shows the track's layout drawn by the community.
- **Races against the AI**: no iRating (no change, no iRating in the results, nothing in the licence summary) and
  "vs AI" on every session, the older ones included.
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.9.8 beta

- **Telemetry like the PC's**: Telemetry, Standings and Tyres. The top card (position, delta, last lap and fuel beside
  the cars ahead and behind, incidents and what is left), the delta bar, the live map with the official turns, the
  relative, fuel and incidents; the standings with every car's gaps and laps, the tyres with their temperatures and
  wear. In DRINKS mode whoever is driving is measured against their own best lap.
- **Analysis like the PC's**: Laps, Coach, My sessions and My races. The coach reads your session's best lap against
  the model's next level (the driver just ahead): the gap, what to work on first, your plan, the map, the sectors and
  the corners that cost the most. The lap analysis compares with your own best, the next level or the record.
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.9.7 beta

- **DRINKS races show who drove every lap**: "DRINKS · Multiple drivers" opens who drove, their laps and their best;
  each lap of the summary says its driver, and your car in the results says who drove it.
- **Races against the AI** say "vs AI", the AI drivers are marked in the results, and those races stay off your
  public profile.
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.9.6 beta

- **The league hub**: every league is a post with the days it races, the usual start in its time zone and in yours,
  its next race, its disciplines (or mixed), its Discord or website and whether it is looking for drivers. Filter by
  discipline, day or open leagues; open a post for its links and a link to share. Post yours and see how many times
  it was opened and its links pressed, day by day.
- **Your licence** on Home is only what the game shows in a session: a race never changes it.
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.9.5 beta

- **The licence summary like the web's**: one card per discipline with its symbol, your iRating, what your last races
  of it gave and your licence.
- **The lap analysis has the corners strip**: every corner as a small bar, touch one to read it on the map and the
  charts; corners are named "T3" like on the PC.
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.9.0 beta

- **The coach reads your racing line**: where you were on the track against the lap you compare with (inside or
  outside at turn-in, apex and exit, in metres), not only where you braked.
- **The track map like iRacing's**: a road with depth, both lines, the official turn numbers and the direction of
  the lap.
- **Licence summary**: the iRating of each category on its own, in its own colour.
- **Driver notes**: tap a driver in a race summary to mark them as dangerous, careful, clean or a friend, with a
  note only you see; the same notes as on the PC and the web.
- "+1L" / "−1L" in the race summary; race rivals show their whole name, as the game shows it.
- The bottom bar is Home, Analysis, Telemetry, Community and Account.
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.8.0 beta

- Fixes and improvements.
